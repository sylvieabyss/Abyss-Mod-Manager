# Abyss Mod Manager 0.101.0 — Windows beta draft

A proprietary Windows beta by Sylvie Abyss. Application source is private; third-party component rights are preserved.

This candidate adds the proprietary application license, privacy policy, third-party notices, a dependency inventory and source-access information. The installer includes a license page; legal documents also accompany the portable distribution.

Includes the launch/draft and profile workflow improvements from0.100.0. RimWorld and Starsector remain the primary tested integrations; other workspaces have differing incomplete capabilities and Antimatter remains deferred. No universal compatibility or performance claim is made.

Download the Setup-x64.exe for installation or Windows-x64.zip for a portable copy, plus SHA256SUMS.txt. Read LICENSE.txt, PRIVACY.md and KNOWN-ISSUES.md. Back up your own saves/mods before testing.

The candidate is unsigned. Do not disable antivirus or other system protections. Packaged/install/source checks do not establish fresh native-game acceptance for this version; older native receipts remain version-specific.

Publication is held until the documented third-party source-access, installer-plugin and Steam integration distribution questions are resolved and the publisher approves this exact public manifest. This is a prepared draft, not an announcement that a release is available.
